Comparisons

Where EmptyInbox fits against the other disposable email APIs, and where it does not. Every page states the cases in which the other tool is the better choice, and every price is checked against the vendor's own pricing page on the date shown.

  • EmptyInbox vs MailSlurp

    A full testing platform from $19.99/mo against a receive-only primitive at $0.10 an inbox. Sending, private domains and SMS are the dividing line.

  • EmptyInbox vs Mailinator

    Mailinator's free inboxes are public and private plans start at $79/mo. EmptyInbox inboxes are private to your API key on every tier.

    EmptyInbox, mail.tm, Mailinator, MailSlurp and Mailosaur on price, privacy, sending and agent support, in one table.
